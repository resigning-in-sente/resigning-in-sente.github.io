### Dong-gyu's Baduk

Jin Dong-gyu 7p is a Korean professional who focuses primarily on educational content.
His [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@%EB%8F%99%EA%B7%9C%EC%9D%98%EB%B0%94%EB%91%91/featured) contains over 2.7k videos (at the time of writing) of valuable information aimed at amateur players, especially ranging from DDK to low dan.
Most of what I write here is ultimately derived from his work.
With his permission, I am adding subtitles to his videos through a website called [Captionify](https://www.captionfy.com/).
This site allows users to add subtitles to YouTube videos.
Views still go to the original creator.
If you like what you see here, please drop by the original channel to drop a like and a sub!
